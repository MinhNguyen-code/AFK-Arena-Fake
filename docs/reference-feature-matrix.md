## 2. BEST-OF REFERENCE DESIGN FOR ETERNAL REALMS 3D

| Feature Area | Source Video(s) | Evidence | Why It Works | How Eternal Realms Will Adapt It | How The Implementation Remains Original |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Camera System** | Videos A, D, G | `CORROBORATED` | A fixed `~34°` FOV portrait isometric-side perspective keeps all 10 fighters and their overhead HP bars inside the central battle viewport without requiring manual panning, while brief subtle zooms on ultimates add spectacle. | Implement a 4-mode 3D camera controller (`STANDARD BATTLE`, `FOCUS CAMERA`, `ULTIMATE CAMERA`, `VICTORY CAMERA`) inside `ThreeBattleCanvas.tsx` with user toggles for `Camera Shake`, `Ultimate Camera`, and `Reduced Motion`. | Uses custom Three.js perspective framing, damped spherical interpolation, and original framing offsets tailored to our 3D arena geometry. |
| **2. Formation & Plinths** | Video D (`167193 VS 221863` ref) | `OBSERVED` / `CORROBORATED` | The 2-frontline / 3-backline staggered layout with glowing octagonal/ring plinths immediately communicates unit roles, frontline protection, and lane targeting. | Map slots `0, 1` to frontline (`x = ±2.35, z = ∓1.55`) and slots `2, 3, 4` to backline (`x = ±4.75, z = -2.7, 0, +2.7`) with 3D faction-colored rune plinths and drag/tap swapping. | Built with procedural Three.js `CylinderGeometry` and `RingGeometry` runic plinths and original Eternal Realms faction emblems. |
| **3. Combat Pacing & Simulation** | Videos A, E, G | `CORROBORATED` | Deterministic tick simulation (`1x`, `2x`, `4x`) with a `90s` battle timer creates a snappy `25–55s` tactical window where frontline durability and ultimate timing matter. | Keep `BattleSimulation` (`src/game/battleEngine.ts`) completely decoupled from rendering, stepping at fixed `dt` and emitting typed `CombatEvent`s consumed by the 3D scene. | Pure original TypeScript combat math, energy curve, faction triangle, and status effect engine tested via `node:test`. |
| **4. 3D Hero Models & Proportions** | Videos A, C, F | `CORROBORATED` | Heroic proportions (readable heads, bold pauldrons, oversized iconic weapons, capes/wings, and clear faction palettes) remain distinct even at mobile portrait scale. | Construct 12 bespoke multi-part hierarchical `THREE.Group` 3D hero rigs (12–16 articulated nodes each) covering Tank, Warrior, Assassin, Archer, Mage, and Support archetypes across all 6 factions. | 100% original procedural 3D geometry and PBR materials (`MeshStandardMaterial` / `MeshPhysicalMaterial`) created in code—zero ripped meshes or sprites. |
