## 1. Evidence & Truthfulness Classification Protocol

Every claim in this audit is strictly classified using one of five evidence labels:

- **OBSERVED:** Directly verified from live YouTube oEmbed/HTML metadata or directly visible in the authentic gameplay frames provided in the project workspace.
- **CORROBORATED:** Confirmed across both the Sorrowh Mobile `"AFK ARENA Gameplay - PART 1..7 (no commentary)"` series metadata and the corresponding AFK Arena Classic battle/hub/formation captures in the workspace.
- **INFERRED:** Logically deduced from the series progression (`PART 1` through `PART 7` continuous no-commentary walkthrough by `Sorrowh Mobile`) and standard AFK Arena Classic campaign unlock rules.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Cannot be confirmed with exact frame-level timestamps because direct raw video stream decoding of YouTube media containers is restricted in the headless container environment.
- **UNAVAILABLE:** Direct frame-by-frame YouTube video stream extraction inside the sandboxed container environment.
